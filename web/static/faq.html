Questions

How it works

How do I play?

Pick a formation and press Start a draft. Each turn deals one real quarter-final squad; take one player into an open place he can play. Twice per draft you can ask for another squad instead. After eleven picks your side plays a tournament: groups of four, two-legged knockouts with extra time and penalties, and a one-night final.

What is Memory mode?

Memory mode hides every rating while you draft, and lists each squad by name instead of best first, so you pick on what you remember about the players. The ratings appear when the tournament starts. Choose it on the home page for a solo draft; in a room, the host chooses it for everyone in the lobby.

Where do the shots, possession and marks come from?

From the game itself. Every match's score is decided first, by an engine whose scoring rates come from real Champions League results. The shots, shots on target, possession, passes and saves are then drawn to fit that score: every goal is a shot on target, and a side that was stronger on paper usually has more of the ball. How many shots a match has, or how accurate the passing is, is this game's design, set by judgement rather than fitted to real data, and the numbers never change a result.

A player's mark out of 10 is the same kind of judgement: 6.4 for a quiet game, more for goals, assists, shots on target, good passing, saves and clean sheets, less for goals conceded, plus a little for winning. Across many tournaments the average mark is about 6.6.

Why can't I take a certain player?

Every player holds up to four positions for that season (a right-back who also played in midfield is "RB · DM"), and a formation is eleven places, each a position: a 4-4-2 has wide midfielders, a 4-3-3 has wingers. He can only take a place for one of his positions, and only if your eleven so far can still all stand somewhere they can play. You never have to plan the order: if a right-back arrives after you took a right-back who also plays midfield, the first one moves into midfield. A man can be in your eleven only once, whichever of his seasons he comes from. The reason is written next to anyone you can't take.

Where do the positions come from?

From 2013–14 they are measured: every position he started at least once for that club that season, in Transfermarkt's line-ups, most-played first, the first four kept. Before that no open source has a position for every match, so they are this game's judgement of the roles he played, as the ratings are, and the archive says which is which. A position played only as a substitute can't be counted, because a line-up doesn't record where a substitute went on.

Why isn't my favourite side in the deck?

The deck holds the quarter-finalists from 1994–95, the first season with a quarter-final round in the Champions League era, to 2025–26. Thirteen of them have no squad list that can be read (the archive lists them at the bottom), and one more, Ajax 1995–96, can't field a side in any of the game's formations.

Where do the ratings come from?

They are this game's judgement, written by hand for each player in each season. See About for why.

How does the daily challenge work?

Every day (it changes at midnight UTC) everyone is dealt the same eleven squads in the same formation, with no rerolls and no ratings: every rating, the opponent's included, stays hidden until you've played, so you pick on what you remember. Your eleven then plays one ninety-minute match against a real side, and there's a goal to reach: win, win by two or more, win without conceding, or score three or more. Each day's goal is measured before it's set: a drafter who always takes the best player reaches it between 30% and 60% of the time, so it's never a formality.

Signed in, you get one ranked attempt a day. The table ranks by the goal, then goal difference, goals scored, the eleven's average mark, and who played first. Signed out, you can play a practice match; it uses a different seed, so it tells you nothing about how the ranked one would go. Your streak lasts until you miss a day.

Can I keep my results?

Yes: finish a tournament signed in and press Save to profile. Daily challenges save themselves. Your profile adds them all up: titles, how far you usually get, your daily streak, and the players who scored and played best for you.

How do rooms with friends work?

Sign in, open Friends and create a room of 2, 4, 8, 16 or 32, then send the invite link. Everyone drafts their own eleven at their own pace from the same shuffle, so you all see the same squads in the same order. Places nobody takes go to real quarter-finalists. When the last eleven is in, the tournament opens for everyone. If someone wanders off, the host can finish their draft for them with the best player each remaining squad offers.

Do I need an account?

Only for rooms, because a room needs to know whose eleven is whose. The solo draft never asks.

I've forgotten my password.

There is no password reset yet: the site doesn't send email. For now, create a new account.

How long does a room last?

A room nobody has started is cleared away after three days. Once a draft has started, the room and its results stay.

Can I put it on my phone's home screen?

Yes. On Android, use your browser's menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It still needs a connection to play.

Is this official?

No. It's an independent fan game, not affiliated with UEFA or any club.
