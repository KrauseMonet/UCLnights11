Questions

How it works

How do I play?

Pick a formation and press Start a draft. Each turn deals one real quarter-final squad; take one player into an open place in his own line. Twice per draft you can ask for another squad instead. After eleven picks your side plays a tournament: groups of four, two-legged knockouts with extra time and penalties, and a one-night final.

What is Memory mode?

Memory mode hides every rating while you draft, and lists each squad by name instead of best first, so you pick on what you remember about the players. The ratings appear when the tournament starts. Choose it on the home page for a solo draft; in a room, the host chooses it for everyone in the lobby.

Why can't I take a certain player?

A player only fills a place in his own line (a defender plays in defence), and a man can only be in your eleven once, whichever of his seasons he comes from. The reason is written next to anyone you can't take.

Why isn't my favourite side in the deck?

The deck holds the quarter-finalists from 1994–95, the first season with a quarter-final round in the Champions League era, to 2025–26. Twelve of them have no squad list that can be read (the archive lists them at the bottom), and AC Milan 2011–12's can only be half read, so it is left out too.

Where do the ratings come from?

They are this game's judgement, written by hand for each player in each season. See About for why.

How do rooms with friends work?

Sign in, open Friends and create a room of 2, 4, 8, 16 or 32, then send the invite link. Everyone drafts their own eleven at their own pace from the same shuffle, so you all see the same squads in the same order. Places nobody takes go to real quarter-finalists. When the last eleven is in, the tournament opens for everyone. If someone wanders off, the host can finish their draft for them with the best player each remaining squad offers.

Do I need an account?

Only for rooms, because a room needs to know whose eleven is whose. The solo draft never asks.

I've forgotten my password.

There is no password reset yet: the site doesn't send email. For now, create a new account.

How long does a room last?

A room nobody has started is cleared away after three days. Once a draft has started, the room and its results stay.

Can I put it on my phone's home screen?

Yes. On Android, use your browser's menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It still needs a connection to play.

Is this official?

No. It's an independent fan game, not affiliated with UEFA or any club.
