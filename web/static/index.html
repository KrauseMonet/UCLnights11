How it plays

Eleven picks, three on the bench, one tournament.

01

The deal

A real quarter-final squad lands in front of you. Bayern reached 24 of them and APOEL one, so some sides come round far more often than others.

02

The pick

Take one player into an open place he played that season, or ask for another squad (twice per draft). The same man can't be picked twice, whichever season he's from. After the eleven, three more for the bench.

03

The nights

Groups of four, then two-legged knockouts with extra time and penalties, and a final at a neutral ground. Thirty-one real sides, each fielding its best eleven.
