✦ Campeones

Thirty-two seasons · every quarter-finalist

Draft a European night

Each turn deals you one real squad that reached the last eight, from Ajax 1994–95 to this year's. Take one player. Eleven turns later your side enters a 32-team tournament against the real thing.

  1. Deal. A random club-season appears. Bayern reached 24 quarter-finals, APOEL one, so some sides are rarer than others.
  2. Pick. Take one player into an open slot in his own line. Two rerolls if a deal does nothing for you.
  3. Play. Groups, then two-legged knockouts, extra time and penalties, and a final at a neutral ground.
