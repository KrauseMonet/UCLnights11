Privacy

What we keep

As little as the game needs. No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies, and nothing loaded from anyone else's servers: the fonts and crests are served from this site.

Playing on your own

A solo draft keeps nothing on our side. The whole draft lives in the page's address, which is why a link to it works and a reload doesn't lose it.

Accounts

An account is only needed to play with friends. It holds:

  • your username, which other players in your rooms see;
  • your email address, used only to sign in. We send no email at all;
  • your password, stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash, never as the password itself;
  • when the account was made.

Saved results and the daily challenge

When you press Save to profile, or play the daily challenge signed in, the site keeps a summary of that tournament or match: how far you got, the score, and your eleven's numbers. Your username and your daily results appear on that day's public table. A practice daily (signed out) is kept nowhere.

Rooms

For each room: who is in it, their formation, and each player's draft so far. The tournament is worked out again from those every time it is shown, so results are not stored separately.

Cookies and your browser

One cookie, ucl_auth, and only once you sign in. It is signed so it can't be forged, can't be read by the page's scripts, and lasts 30 days or until you sign out.

Your browser's local storage remembers a few choices on your own device: your formation, whether you like Memory mode, the room size you last picked, and how far you have watched each tournament. None of it is sent to us.

When a page breaks

If a page hits an error, your browser sends us the error message and the page's path (for example /room) so we can fix it. Nothing that identifies you is included, and reports are deleted after 30 days.

Where it lives

The site runs on Vercel (its server code in Mumbai) and the accounts and rooms database on Neon (in Singapore). Like any host, Vercel keeps short-lived request logs, which include IP addresses, under its own policies.

Deleting your account

There is no button for it yet. Open an issue on the project's GitHub page with your username (never your password or email), and the account and its seats in rooms will be removed.
