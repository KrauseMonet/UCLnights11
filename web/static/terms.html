Ratings are this game's opinion, and every match is a simulation. Neither says anything about how a real player or side would do.

Your account

Keep your password to yourself, pick a username you'd be happy for your friends to see, and don't use the site to harass anyone. Accounts used to abuse the site or other players may be removed.
